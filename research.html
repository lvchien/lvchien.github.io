Core areas

Research interests

01

Computational electromagnetics

Stable and efficient boundary integral equation methods for electromagnetic scattering problems.

02

PDEs and multiphysics

Analysis and simulation of induction heating, moving interfaces, mechanics, and fluid dynamics.

03

Numerical analysis

FEM, BEM, coupled FEM-BEM, space-time methods, shape optimization, and optimal control.

04

Scientific computing

Operator preconditioning, iterative solvers, and domain decomposition.
