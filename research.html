Research

Mathematical structure, numerical stability, physical insight.

My research combines rigorous analysis of PDEs and boundary integral equations with the design and implementation of robust computational methods.

Research areas, recognition, funded work, and invited lectures.

Core areas

Research interests

01

Computational electromagnetics

Stable MFIE, CFIE, PMCHWT, and Müller formulations across frequency and time domains.

02

PDEs and multiphysics

Analysis and simulation of induction heating, moving interfaces, mechanics, and fluid dynamics.

03

Numerical analysis

FEM, BEM, operator preconditioning, quasi-Helmholtz decompositions, and space–time methods.
